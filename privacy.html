Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy covers the personal, self-hosted Rakazo instance described on the home page. The instance is used only by its owner.

What data is accessed

When the owner connects their Google account, the app receives OAuth tokens and, on the owner's request, accesses the owner's Gmail data (messages, labels, drafts) through the Gmail API.

How data is used

Gmail data is used only to provide the assistant features the owner asks for, such as searching, summarizing, drafting and organizing email. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI models.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and any stored content are kept encrypted on a private server controlled by the owner and reachable only over a private network. When the owner asks, parts of the content are sent to the AI model provider the owner configured, only to answer that request.

Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties, except the model provider described above as needed to process the owner's requests.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting the integration removes stored tokens from the instance.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/jorgemasta/rakazo/issues.
